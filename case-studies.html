Healthcare · Dental

Turning one dentist into a content system, without adding a content team.

A multi-location dental practice wanted to educate patients and build trust online, but the dentist had no time to film, write, or post. We built an AI avatar system trained on her voice and expertise so she could produce a steady stream of patient-education video content without ever picking up a camera.

The Challenge

Zero bandwidth for content, but real demand for patient trust and visibility.

What We Built

A trained AI avatar and repeatable video pipeline, from script to finished post.

The Result

A consistent content presence the practice never had to staff for.

Healthcare · Mental Health

HIPAA-aware booking for a solo therapy practice, done right the first time.

A solo mental health private practice needed online booking that actually met HIPAA expectations, not a generic scheduling widget bolted onto a website. We built a compliant booking and intake flow, paired with AI-assisted follow-up so nothing slipped through the cracks between sessions.

The Challenge

Needed real booking and payments without compromising patient privacy.

What We Built

A HIPAA-aware booking flow with AI-assisted intake and reminders.

The Result

A fully booked calendar and zero manual scheduling back-and-forth.

Growing Business · Solo Founder

AI 101 for a founder who'd never touched these tools before.

A growing solo-entrepreneur brand wanted to use AI in her day-to-day work but didn't know where to start. Rather than hand her software and walk away, we built out a hands-on onboarding path and lightweight content systems so she could actually learn the tools, not just have them running in the background.

The Challenge

Wanted to use AI, not just have it done for her.

What We Built

Guided AI 101 onboarding plus a simple content system she runs herself.

The Result

A founder who's now confident enough to use AI without us in the room. This one's still early, more to come.
