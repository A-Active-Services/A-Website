# Design Philosophy

The website should feel like a premium consulting company—not an AI-generated template.

## Avoid

- Generic AI artwork
- Robots
- Neon cyberpunk effects
- Floating blobs
- Random gradients
- Overused AI illustrations
- Cheap stock imagery
- Template layouts

## Instead

Take inspiration from:

- Apple
- Stripe
- Notion
- Linear
- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Vercel
- Figma

The experience should feel intentional, elegant, editorial, and timeless.

# Visual Direction

Follow the official A+ Active Services Brand Guide.

Use:
- Large typography
- Lots of white space
- Clean grids
- Editorial layouts
- Rounded corners
- Subtle animations
- Premium photography
- High contrast
- Consistent spacing

The overall aesthetic should resemble a modern SaaS company with luxury consulting aesthetics.

---

# Photography

Use authentic imagery.

Real people.

Healthcare.

Business meetings.

Technology.

Education.

Collaboration.

Avoid fake smiling call-center stock photos.

Avoid futuristic robots.

---

# Motion

Animations should be subtle.

Fast.

Smooth.

Elegant.

Purposeful.

Performance always comes before visual effects.

# Golden Rule For Claude Code

Whenever there are multiple design options, choose the one that is:

- More timeless
- More premium
- More human
- More intentional
- Less cluttered
- Less trendy
- Less AI-generated

If something looks like a template, redesign it.

Every design decision should reinforce trust, professionalism, and long-term credibility.

The website should feel handcrafted.
